Graph models

Dimensions describe one zone, observation or directed pair unless the diagram shows a whole graph. Neural preprocessing uses training statistics and appends a missingness bit for each input field. The runners convert each head’s output s to a price in EUR/MWh with 50 × sinh(s).

France in the static graph

Calendar and both price lags enter the zone state. BE, CH, DE_LU, ES and IT_NORD send incoming physical-link messages. The flat-CORE variant also gets 107 presolved constraints, minimum RAM/Fmax 0.199769 and a domain-present flag.

France in the bipartite graph

The same flat-CORE features enter the zone state. The historical domain contributes 113 constraint nodes. France exchanges messages with 107 of them through finite nonzero PTDFs. The other six published French PTDFs are zero.
