\paragraph{Graph contribution.} Real GAT improves test MAE by 0.21 (1.00\%) against MLP, but only 0.09 against self-loop GAT and 0.08 against pooled XGBoost. Pooled XGBoost has the best validation score among richer-input controls; the small test advantage does not justify selecting GAT retrospectively. MLP-to-GAT validation improvement is 0.46 (95\% paired week interval [0.20, 0.86]). Weeks are shared across countries, conditional on fitted models. Real GAT beats all three shuffled means on test; one shuffled-control validation interval includes zero.
\paragraph{Earlier and extended experiments.} The first single-seed real/shuffled GAT scores of 20.83/21.54 become 20.97/21.45 across seeds and rewirings. Pooled rich XGBoost scores 21.06 versus 22.11 for per-zone models. Gains vary: France GAT scores 25.48 versus MLP's 22.00. Weather-only GAT has test MAE 20.90, but richer GAT has better validation MAE (20.98 versus 21.06). Load and JAO effects are mixed. GRU--MLP/GAT score 24.50/23.59, worse than static controls; these settings do not rule out other temporal models or detailed constraints.

\paragraph{Timing and limitations.} Prior-day auction prices are normally published on $D-2$ \cite{auction}; DST guards exclude lag-24 target-day prices. ERA5-derived lag-48 inputs conflict with documented publication latency \cite{era5}; load/JAO vintages remain unresolved. Temporal histories end by noon. Static training-only topology does not reconstruct commissioning dates. All models retain identical target keys/masks and missing features; targets are never filled. Previously inspected test data make these conditional exploratory comparisons, not confirmatory evidence. Multiple feature comparisons do not establish causal effects.

\section{Future work} Build a daily forecast service that saves predictions before Brussels noon, together with received inputs and model versions. Score forecasts when auction results arrive; monitor rolling rMAE, data completeness, deadline misses and fallback use. Start with price-only pooled XGBoost and persistence, then compare MLP/GAT and timely station observations. This prospective experiment measures accuracy, drift and operational reliability. Assess detailed constraint graphs after establishing the baseline service.
